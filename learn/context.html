Tracks and steps

So far every function worked on “the track”: the current one, the track you were on when you opened the menu. tr.add 5 never says which track, and that is on purpose. Operations act on a context: a set of tracks, a set of steps, and a source of chance. This study changes the context to write a beat on three tracks at once.

Choosing the tracks: trk

trk takes one to eight track numbers, or cur (the current track), or all. Like a modifier, it governs the rest of its line or the block below:

With several tracks, OType runs what trk governs once per track, in the order written: trk 1 2 3: tr.clr clears track 1, then 2, then 3. Everything inside a pass, a loop or a random draw, runs again for the next track.

For the rest of the block

trk can also stand alone, without a colon. It then sets the tracks until the end of the block it is in:

Either way, a block hands its context back when it ends: after trk 2: and its block, the next line is on the tracks it was on before.

Choosing the steps: steps and step

The context also holds a set of steps, at first every step of the pattern. steps changes it:

writethe steps become
steps allevery step
steps 9 16steps 9 to 16
steps has.trigthose of the current steps that hold a trig
steps is.emptythose of the current steps that are empty
step 5step 5 alone

A range replaces the steps; a test such as has.trig narrows the ones you already have. Operations that work on many steps at once, and each:, use this set:

each: visits the steps that hold a trig, and dr.v 1 i reads curve 1, accents on the beats, at step i.

Locks go where the trigs are

On the Octatrack, a lock belongs to a trig. OType follows the machine: a lock written on an empty step is skipped, and the simulator counts it.

To lock a step without playing it, first make it a trigless lock: tl.add s.

A function inherits the context

When one of your functions calls another, the called function starts with a copy of the caller's context: the same tracks and steps. Whatever it changes stays inside it. This is what lets the next study write a function once and use it on any track.

Try it

  • Put the snare of beat on track 2 with a single if inside a loop.
  • Lock a random volume on every trig of tracks 1 to 4 with steps has.trig and rrand 60 127.
  • Use steps 9 16 to clear only the second half of the bar with tr.clr.
